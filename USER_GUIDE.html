Getting Started

Two permissions. That's all.

RemindMe requires Location and Notification access to work reliably in the background.

Location Access
  • Tap Allow While Using App first.
  • When the second prompt appears, tap Always Allow — this keeps monitoring running when the app is closed.
Why "Always"? iOS stops geofence monitoring when the app only has "When In Use" access. Always access is the only way reminders fire reliably in the background.
Notification Access

A notification prompt appears at the same time. Tap Allow to receive reminder alerts.

Permission Status

Check both permissions anytime under Settings → Permissions.

Creating a Reminder

Seven steps to your first reminder.

From title to location to alert type — everything is on one screen.

1

Open the Add Screen

Tap + in the top-right corner of the Home screen, or tap New Reminder at the bottom of the list.

2

Enter Title and Description

  • Title (required) — e.g. "When arriving home"
  • Description (optional) — shown in the notification body
3

Pick a Location

A — Tap the map to drop a pin at any exact point.

B — Search for an address or place name in the bar above the map.

C — Select a Saved Location from your favourites library.

4

Set the Trigger Radius

Drag the slider to choose how close you need to be. The map zooms automatically to frame the circle.

Saved Locations

Your favourite places, always ready.

Save up to 20 places and reuse them when creating reminders — no searching every time.

Adding a Saved Location
  1. Tap the Bookmarks tab at the bottom
  2. Tap Add Location
  3. Search for a place or tap the map to pin it
  4. Give it a name — e.g. "Home", "Office", "Gym"
  5. Tap Save
Using in a Reminder

When creating a reminder, tap Select Saved Location. A sheet slides up with your saved places. A checkmark shows the currently selected one.

Editing or Deleting

Notifications

How your reminders reach you.

How a reminder fires
  1. You enter (or exit) the radius of an active reminder
  2. iOS detects the geofence boundary crossing
  3. RemindMe sends a notification with the title and description
  4. If Repeat Notification is on, follow-up alerts fire at your chosen interval until you open one
Tapping a notification

Opens the app directly to that reminder's detail page, regardless of what was open.

Tips & Tricks

Get more out of RemindMe.

🎯
Tight radius for precise spots
Use 50–100 m for a specific shop or building. A larger radius may fire too early.
🚨
Urgent for truly critical tasks
Urgent reminders bypass Do Not Disturb. Use them sparingly so they stay meaningful.
🔖
Save your most-visited places
Add Home, Work, and your regular spots once. Creating reminders for them then takes seconds.
🔁
Repeat for important tasks
If you dismiss notifications without acting, turn on Repeat with a 5-minute interval.
⏸
Deactivate instead of delete
Seasonal reminders can be deactivated and reactivated later — no need to recreate them.
📊
Check your geofence count
iOS allows only 20 active geofences per app. Check Settings → Permissions to see usage.
FAQ

Common questions.

Why didn't my reminder fire?
Check: (1) reminder is marked Active, (2) Location is set to Always in Settings → Permissions, (3) Precise Location is enabled (not Approximate) in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services, (4) you haven't exceeded the 20-active-reminder limit, (5) radius isn't too small for a moving vehicle — use at least 200 m on motorways.
Why does RemindMe need "Always On" location access?
iOS only processes geofences in the background if the app has Always access. With "When In Use" only, monitoring stops the moment you leave the app. Always access does not mean RemindMe is constantly tracking you — it only watches the geofence boundaries you set up.
Does RemindMe drain my battery?
RemindMe uses CoreLocation's geofencing API, which is battery-efficient. It relies on cell tower and Wi-Fi positioning for coarse boundary checks and only activates GPS when you're near a boundary. Impact is typically minimal.
Can I have more than 20 reminders?
You can save unlimited reminders, but only 20 can be active at the same time — this is an iOS system limit. Deactivate or mark done any reminders you no longer need to free up slots.
What happens to my data?
All reminders and saved locations are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.
The app fired a reminder twice — why?
There are two separate reasons:

Repeat Notification is ON — this is intentional. The app schedules follow-up notifications at your chosen interval (1, 2, 5, or 10 min) after the initial trigger, until you open one. They fire even if you haven't moved. Adjust in Settings → Notifications.

Repeat Notification is OFF — RemindMe has a built-in 5-minute cooldown between geofence triggers to prevent double-firing when you hover near a boundary. If you receive two alerts more than 5 minutes apart with this setting off, you genuinely crossed the boundary twice.
Can I use RemindMe offline?
Yes. Geofencing uses your device's location hardware and doesn't require an internet connection. The only feature that needs connectivity is the place search when creating a reminder.

Notifications

How your reminders reach you.

How a reminder fires
  1. You enter (or exit) the radius of an active reminder
  2. iOS detects the geofence boundary crossing
  3. RemindMe sends a notification with the title and description
  4. If Repeat Notification is on, follow-up alerts fire at your chosen interval until you open one

Tips & Tricks

Get more out of RemindMe.

🎯
Tight radius for precise spots
Use 50–100 m for a specific shop or building. A larger radius may fire too early.
🚨
Urgent for truly critical tasks
Urgent reminders bypass Do Not Disturb. Use them sparingly so they stay meaningful.
🔖
Save your most-visited places
Add Home, Work, and your regular spots once. Creating reminders for them then takes seconds.
🔁
Repeat for important tasks
If you dismiss notifications without acting, turn on Repeat with a 5-minute interval.
⏸
Deactivate instead of delete
Seasonal reminders can be deactivated and reactivated later — no need to recreate them.
📊
Check your geofence count
iOS allows only 20 active geofences per app. Check Settings → Permissions to see usage.
FAQ

Common questions.

Why didn't my reminder fire?
Check: (1) reminder is marked Active, (2) Location is set to Always in Settings → Permissions, (3) Precise Location is enabled (not Approximate) in iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services, (4) you haven't exceeded the 20-active-reminder limit, (5) radius isn't too small for a moving vehicle — use at least 200 m on motorways.
Why does RemindMe need "Always On" location access?
iOS only processes geofences in the background if the app has Always access. With "When In Use" only, monitoring stops the moment you leave the app. Always access does not mean RemindMe is constantly tracking you — it only watches the geofence boundaries you set up.
Does RemindMe drain my battery?
RemindMe uses CoreLocation's geofencing API, which is battery-efficient. It relies on cell tower and Wi-Fi positioning for coarse boundary checks and only activates GPS when you're near a boundary. Impact is typically minimal.
Can I have more than 20 reminders?
You can save unlimited reminders, but only 20 can be active at the same time — this is an iOS system limit. Deactivate or mark done any reminders you no longer need to free up slots.
What happens to my data?
All reminders and saved locations are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.
The app fired a reminder twice — why?
There are two separate reasons:

Repeat Notification is ON — this is intentional. The app schedules follow-up notifications at your chosen interval (1, 2, 5, or 10 min) after the initial trigger, until you open one. They fire even if you haven't moved. Adjust in Settings → Notifications.

Repeat Notification is OFF — RemindMe has a built-in 5-minute cooldown between geofence triggers to prevent double-firing when you hover near a boundary. If you receive two alerts more than 5 minutes apart with this setting off, you genuinely crossed the boundary twice.
Can I use RemindMe offline?
Yes. Geofencing uses your device's location hardware and doesn't require an internet connection. The only feature that needs connectivity is the place search when creating a reminder.
