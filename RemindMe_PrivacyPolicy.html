RemindMe

Privacy Policy — Effective date: May 14, 2025

Overview

RemindMe is a location-based reminder app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data We Collect

RemindMe does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All reminders, locations, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Location Access

The app requests Always On location permission so it can monitor geofences and trigger reminders when you arrive at a saved place — even when the app is in the background or closed. Your location data is processed entirely on-device and is never sent to any server or third party.

Notifications

The app sends local notifications triggered by your device's location. These notifications are generated on-device; no notification content is transmitted to any external service.

No Third-Party SDKs or Analytics

RemindMe contains no advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or crash-reporting libraries that would send data off your device.

Children's Privacy

RemindMe does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
